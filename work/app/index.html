About this model & keyboard controls

A simplified teaching model of a resettable, three-dial luggage lock. Geometry and travel are exaggerated for clarity; real locks use different latch and reset arrangements. The release is held inward while open. A return spring is seated between the base and carriage. A stop on the carriage blocks the reset yoke until the carriage is open. Stationary thrust bearings and close-fitting casing windows retain each number dial axially. The reset yoke engages grooves in the inner sleeve hubs and slides those sleeves along the shaft. Wide fence fingers keep the notches aligned throughout this travel, allowing the fixed-position dials to rotate independently. During reset, a broad tongue on the yoke slides behind the carriage stop and physically blocks its return. The tongue engages before the clutches separate and clears only after they re-engage. Click the release while setting to test this stop. The reset pin remains held inward until you click it again to save. Exploded view removes the casing and separates the wheel group for static inspection; it is not a disassembly sequence. The fixed catch represents what the latch engages; no zipper or pullers are shown.

Use Tab to move between controls. Tab to a number wheel on the model, then use ↑ / ↓ or type 0–9. Shift-click turns a wheel backward. Use the view buttons, zoom buttons, and Restore camera view without dragging. Reset: click the release to open → click the reset pin → turn the wheels → click the reset pin to save → click the release to close. Turn a wheel manually to block opening again.
